## RESEARCH SYNTHESIS

### Best-in-Class Simulator Analysis (2024-2026)

#### **Farming Simulator 25** (Nov 2025, 6M+ copies sold)
**Addictive Loops:**
- **Zen workflows:** "Odd zen to just towing a cultivator" — meditative, satisfying work loops
- **Harvest → prepare → serve → upgrade cycle** that creates time loss ("lose track of time")
- **Mod ecosystem:** 3.2 BILLION downloads = infinite replayability
- **Real brands:** Authenticity creates emotional investment
- **Seasonal cycles:** Time pressure without stress

**Key Takeaway:** FS25 just added "Highlands Fishing" DLC with basic salmon aquaculture. **It's surface-level.** Our opportunity: be 100x deeper. FS25 aquaculture is "plant → harvest." We are the full biological/economic/regulatory ecosystem.

#### **Anno 1800** (Still #1 city builder 2024-2025)
**Addictive Loops:**
- **Escalating production chains:** "Vicious cycle of escalating citizen demands and ever more complex production chains"
- **Optional complexity:** "Everything totally works if you just plop down some chains... BUT you can optimize endlessly"
- **Layered progression:** Simple at start, overwhelmingly deep at endgame
- **Visual feedback:** See your empire grow physically
- **Multiple regions:** Different challenges keep gameplay fresh

**Key Takeaway:** Start simple, add layers gradually. Show the growth visually. Make optimization OPTIONAL but rewarding.

#### **Frostpunk 2** (2024 hit)
**Addictive Loops:**
- **Political Council system:** Not just building — navigating factions and pressure
- **Survival tension:** Cold protection creates constant pressure
- **Moral dilemmas:** "Children went feral" = emergent storytelling

**Key Takeaway:** Add political/social systems for depth beyond pure economics.

#### **Manor Lords** (2024 Early Access phenomenon)
**Addictive Loops:**
- **Gridless placement:** Full creative freedom = player ownership
- **Organic growth:** Cities grow like real medieval towns (routes + landscape)
- **Relaxed pacing option:** "Some of us find enjoyment in complacent games"

**Key Takeaway:** Offer difficulty modes. Not everyone wants constant stress.

### Salmon Farming Industry Trends (2026)

#### **Technology Revolution**
1. **AI-driven operations** (EY Norway 2025): "Moving from manual oversight to automatic model-based systems"
   - **Projected impact:** +35% production efficiency by 2026 (Farmonaut)
   - **Real-time sensors:** IoT monitoring of O2, temp, pH, salinity, ammonia
   - **Predictive analytics:** Prevent disease outbreaks, optimize feeding
   - **Automated feeding systems:** Analyze fish behavior, reduce waste

2. **Semi-Closed/Closed Containment Systems**
   - **Prevent escapes** (major regulatory concern)
   - **Eliminate sea lice problems** (biggest operational headache)
   - **Controlled environment** = less weather/disease risk

3. **RAS (Recirculating Aquaculture Systems)**
   - **Land-based farming** with minimal water use
   - Growing trend for sustainability compliance

#### **Sustainability Pressures** (Norway's 2026 White Paper)
1. **New lice quota system:** "Total lice quota based on environment's natural limit" — <10% wild salmon mortality target
2. **Traffic light system evolution:** Implementation postponed to 2026+, but coming
3. **Resource rent tax:** Transition from volume-based to impact-based regulation
4. **Feed sustainability:** Shift to insect meal, single-cell proteins, microalgae (away from fishmeal)

#### **Market Trends**
- **Premium markets growing:** Japan sashimi-grade (+29% premium in our intl markets system)
- **Transparency demands:** Supply chain tracking, traceability
- **ESG investing:** Sustainability ratings affect capital access

## 🎯 TOP 5 NEXT FEATURES

### **FEATURE 1: Technology Research Tree 2.0 ("The Innovation Path")**

#### **Rationale:**
**Gameplay Value:**
- **Long-term progression hook** — Anno 1800's "unlock next tier" addiction
- **Strategic choices** — Tech tree branches = player identity
- **Mastery curve** — Knowledge compounds over playthroughs
- **"One more research" loop** — Always something completing soon

**Industry Relevance:**
- **AI/automation is THE 2026 trend** (+35% efficiency projected)
- **RAS systems** are revolutionizing land-based farming
- **Sensor tech** is moving from manual to predictive operations
- **Feed innovations** (insect meal, algae) are sustainability game-changers

#### **Implementation:**
**4 Tech Branches:**

1. **BIOLOGY BRANCH:**
   - Tier 1: Basic Genetics (current system)
   - Tier 2: Genomic Selection (marker-assisted breeding)
   - Tier 3: CRISPR Gene Editing (disease immunity, growth hacking)
   - Tier 4: Synthetic Biology (custom traits, bio-engineered lines)

2. **AUTOMATION BRANCH:**
   - Tier 1: Manual Operations (current)
   - Tier 2: Auto-Feeders (reduce labor, optimize timing)
   - Tier 3: AI Monitoring (predictive disease alerts, O2 warnings)
   - Tier 4: Full Automation (lights-out operation, 50% labor reduction)

3. **INFRASTRUCTURE BRANCH:**
   - Tier 1: Traditional Net Pens (current)
   - Tier 2: Semi-Closed Containment (reduce lice 60%, prevent escapes)
   - Tier 3: Offshore Ocean Farms (deep water, storm-resistant)
   - Tier 4: Land-Based RAS (zero escapes, zero lice, but high CAPEX)

4. **SUSTAINABILITY BRANCH:**
   - Tier 1: Standard Feed (fishmeal-based)
   - Tier 2: Insect Meal Blend (10% carbon reduction, cost neutral)
   - Tier 3: Algae/Single-Cell Proteins (30% carbon reduction, +5% premium)
   - Tier 4: Circular Economy (zero-waste, byproduct monetization)

**Research Mechanics:**
- **Unlocks via:** Revenue milestones + industry partnerships + government grants
- **Research time:** Real-time countdown (speed up with investment)
- **Branching choices:** Can't have EVERYTHING — encourages replay
- **Failed experiments:** 10% chance of setback = tension
- **Patent licensing:** Unlock tech for competitors (revenue stream) or keep exclusive

**UI/UX:**
- Beautiful tech tree visualization (Civilization-style)
- 3D previews of new equipment
- Before/after efficiency comparisons
- "Research Complete!" celebration moment

#### **Priority:** **P0** (Foundation for future depth)  
#### **Complexity:** **LARGE** (4-6 weeks, C# backend + GDScript UI + JSON data + balancing)

### **FEATURE 2: Sustainability Score & Green Certification ("The Conscience Mechanic")**

#### **Rationale:**
**Gameplay Value:**
- **Positive engagement loop** — Currently sustainability = penalties only (boring!)
- **Player identity:** "I'm the eco-friendly farmer" vs "I'm the profit maximizer"
- **New market unlock:** Premium buyers (Whole Foods, Waitrose) require ASC/Organic
- **Visual feedback:** Green/yellow/red farm status = satisfaction/shame
- **Social sharing:** "My farm is 95% sustainable!"

**Industry Relevance:**
- **2026 Norway White Paper:** Lice quotas, environmental limits = core regulation
- **ESG investing:** Sustainability ratings affect capital access (real mechanic!)
- **Consumer demand:** Premium markets pay +12-29% for certified sustainable
- **Real certifications:** ASC (Aquaculture Stewardship Council), Organic, BAP

#### **Implementation:**
**Sustainability Score (0-100):**

**Score Components:**
1. **Sea Lice Management (25 points)**
   - <0.2 female lice/fish = 25 pts
   - 0.2-0.5 = 15 pts
   - >0.5 = 0 pts
   - Bonus: +5 pts for mechanical delousing (no chemicals)

2. **Escape Prevention (20 points)**
   - Zero escapes/year = 20 pts
   - 1-10 escapes = 10 pts
   - >10 = 0 pts
   - Bonus: +5 pts for closed containment systems

3. **Feed Sustainability (20 points)**
   - 100% sustainable sources = 20 pts
   - Insect meal/algae use = +5 bonus
   - Fishmeal from overfished stocks = -10 penalty

4. **Carbon Footprint (15 points)**
   - Renewable energy use (solar, wind, tidal)
   - Transport efficiency (local processing vs shipping)
   - Vessel fuel type (diesel vs hybrid vs electric)

5. **Welfare Standards (10 points)**
   - Stocking density below max
   - Handling stress minimization
   - Slaughter methods (electrical stunning best practice)

6. **Ecosystem Impact (10 points)**
   - Benthic monitoring (seabed health under pens)
   - Nutrient discharge levels
   - Wild fish attraction/displacement

**Certification Tiers:**
- **0-49:** Non-compliant (can't export to EU, fines likely)
- **50-69:** Basic Compliance (standard markets only)
- **70-84:** ASC Certified (unlock premium buyers +8% price)
- **85-94:** Organic Certified (unlock specialty markets +15% price)
- **95-100:** Sustainability Leader (ESG investor bonuses, media awards, government grants)

**Gameplay Mechanics:**
- **Quarterly audits:** Random inspections (can fail certification if caught violating)
- **Improvement investments:** Solar panels, better nets, RAS upgrades
- **Trade-offs:** High sustainability = higher costs BUT premium markets + grants offset
- **Competitor pressure:** If rivals go green, market expects YOU to also
- **Reputation system:** Scandals tank score, recovery takes time

**Visual Feedback:**
- Farm color-coded on map (green/yellow/red)
- Certificate badges in UI
- News headlines: "Lakskongen Wins Sustainability Award" vs "Eco Group Protests Farm"
- Dynamic fjord health visualization (clear water vs algae blooms)

**Monetization of Sustainability:**
- **Premium market access:** Whole Foods, Waitrose, Japan Premium = +12-29% margins
- **Government grants:** Norway subsidizes green tech (offset 30% of RAS investment)
- **ESG investment:** Lower interest rates on loans if 80+ score
- **Brand value:** Unlock export markets that require certification

#### **Priority:** **P0** (Differentiator vs FS25 shallow aquaculture)  
#### **Complexity:** **MEDIUM** (2-3 weeks, leverages existing disease/logistics systems)

**Gameplay Value:**
- **Player expression:** "This is MY beautiful fjord farm"

- **Norwegian fjords = STUNNING** (natural marketing advantage)
- **Aquaculture is photogenic:** Underwater shots, jumping salmon, Northern Lights
- **Educational outreach:** Beautiful visuals attract schools/institutions

#### **Implementation:**
**Photo Mode Features:**
1. **Free camera:** Detach from gameplay, fly anywhere
2. **Filters:** Golden hour, storm drama, underwater, Northern Lights, seasonal palettes
3. **HUD toggle:** Clean screenshots without UI clutter
4. **Depth of field:** Tilt-shift miniature effect (Anno 1800 style)
5. **Time control:** Pause, rewind 30 sec, fast-forward for time-lapse
6. **Focal length:** Wide-angle landscape vs close-up detail shots
7. **Weather override:** Force sunset/storm/aurora for dramatic effect

**Visual Milestones (Automatic Cinematics):**
- **First Harvest:** Slow-mo net lift, fish spilling into well-boat, music swell
- **1 Million NOK Revenue:** Champagne cork pop, fireworks over fjord
- **Perfect Sustainability Quarter:** Drone shot of pristine farm, eco-badge animation
- **Crisis Survival:** Storm aftermath → recovery montage → thriving farm (15-sec video)
- **Empire Achievement:** Multi-site overview, vertical integration visualization

**Sharing Integration:**
- **One-click export:** Steam screenshot folder, auto-upload to Workshop
- **Watermark option:** "Built in Lakskongen" branding (optional)
- **Video export:** 10-second clips for TikTok/Instagram/YouTube Shorts
- **Screenshot challenges:** Weekly theme ("Best Storm Shot," "Underwater Beauty")

**Creator Tools:**
- **Replay system:** Record last 5 minutes, export timelapse
- **Camera paths:** Set keyframes for cinematic panning shots
- **Custom overlays:** Add farm stats, logos, text to screenshots

#### **Priority:** **P1** (Retention + marketing multiplier)  
#### **Complexity:** **SMALL** (1-2 weeks, Godot camera system + export API)

### **FEATURE 4: Active Competitor AI Behaviors ("The Rival Mechanic")**

#### **Rationale:**
**Gameplay Value:**
- **Dynamic world:** Currently competitors are static profiles — BORING
- **Emergent stories:** "Mowi just undercut me on contracts!" = memorable moment
- **Strategic pressure:** Can't just optimize in vacuum — rivals adapt
- **Crisis opportunities:** Competitor bankruptcy = acquisition chance (feeds Crisis Capitalism system)
- **Multiplayer-lite feel:** Single-player but feels like PvP

**Industry Relevance:**
- **Norwegian salmon industry IS competitive:** Mowi, SalMar, Grieg, Lerøy battle for market share
- **Real M&A activity:** Companies buy distressed rivals constantly
- **Price wars:** Spot market competition drives strategy
- **Technology races:** Who adopts RAS/AI/offshore first gains advantage

#### **Implementation:**
**AI Competitor Actions (Monthly Turn-Based):**

1. **Market Behaviors:**
   - **Spot Market Flooding:** Dump inventory to crash prices (hurt all players)
   - **Premium Positioning:** Invest in sustainability to steal premium contracts
   - **Export Expansion:** Enter new markets (reduces your margins there)
   - **Contract Bidding:** Compete for exclusive deals (lockout mechanic)

2. **Expansion Behaviors:**
   - **License Acquisition:** Buy MAB licenses in your zones (increase competition)
   - **Technology Adoption:** Invest in RAS/AI (if you don't, they gain edge)
   - **Vertical Integration:** Buy feed mills/processing (reduce your options)
   - **New Site Development:** Expand to new fjords

3. **Crisis Behaviors:**
   - **Disease Outbreak Response:** Quarantine, emergency harvest (market impact)
   - **Financial Distress:** Go bankrupt → acquisition opportunity
   - **Regulatory Violations:** Get fined/shut down (market share opens up)
   - **Storm Damage:** Slow recovery vs fast recovery (AI personality-driven)

4. **Strategic Behaviors (AI Personality-Driven):**
   - **Aggressive (Mowi-type):** M&A focused, price wars, rapid expansion
   - **Conservative (Family Farm):** Slow growth, high quality, risk-averse
   - **Environmental (Grieg-type):** Sustainability leader, premium markets
   - **Innovator (SalMar-type):** Tech-first, offshore pioneers
   - **Integrated (Lerøy-type):** Vertical integration, control full value chain

**Player Visibility:**
- **News Feed System:** "SalMar Invests 450M NOK in New RAS Facility"
- **Market Impact Notifications:** "Competitor price drop detected! Your margins -12%"
- **Intelligence Reports:** Quarterly briefings on rival activities
- **Visual Map Updates:** See competitor sites appear/expand/close

**Dynamic Events from AI:**
- **Price Wars:** Competitor dumps inventory, player must match or lose sales
- **Technology Arms Race:** Rival adopts AI feeding, player efficiency gap widens
- **Distress Acquisitions:** Bankrupt competitor triggers Crisis Capitalism opportunity
- **Contract Theft:** Lose exclusive deal when rival offers better terms

**Balance Mechanics:**
- **AI difficulty scales:** Harder = smarter/faster rivals
- **Regional focus:** Some competitors regional, others national (different threat levels)
- **Transparent logic:** Player can learn AI patterns and counter-strategize
- **Fairness:** AI doesn't cheat, uses same economic rules

#### **Priority:** **P1** (Brings static world to life)  
#### **Complexity:** **MEDIUM** (3-4 weeks, C# AI logic + event triggers + UI notifications)

### **FEATURE 5: Campaign Scenarios + Sandbox Mode ("The Replayability System")**

#### **Rationale:**
**Gameplay Value:**
- **Structured learning:** Campaign = guided tour of all systems
- **Emergent stories:** Scenarios create memorable narratives
- **Replayability:** Each scenario = different playstyle encouraged
- **Achievement hunting:** Scenario-specific goals = long-term engagement
- **Difficulty range:** Tutorial → Normal → Expert → Nightmare modes
- **Speedrun potential:** Streamer/community content creation

**Industry Relevance:**
- **Real aquaculture challenges:** Each scenario based on industry case study
- **Educational value:** Teaches real salmon farming strategies
- **Norwegian authenticity:** Scenarios set in real regions with local challenges

#### **Implementation:**
**CAMPAIGN MODE (8 Scenarios):**

**1. "Heritage Farm" (Tutorial)**
- **Setting:** Family farm in Hardanger, 1 license, aging infrastructure
- **Goal:** Survive 2 years, earn 5M NOK, pass farm to next generation
- **Teaches:** Basic biology, feeding, harvest timing, simple market sales
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Unlock:** None (starting scenario)

**2. "The Startup" (Normal)**
- **Setting:** New entrepreneur, 1 license in Trøndelag, bank loan
- **Goal:** Break even in 18 months, achieve 60% sustainability score
- **Teaches:** Financial management, loan repayment, sustainability basics
- **Difficulty:** Normal
- **Unlock:** Complete "Heritage Farm"

**3. "Storm Survivor" (Crisis Management)**
- **Setting:** Established farm hit by massive winter storm (Dec 2024 real event)
- **Goal:** Recover from 40% infrastructure damage, prevent bankruptcy
- **Teaches:** Emergency response, insurance, crisis cash flow management
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Unlock:** Complete "The Startup"

**4. "Disease Outbreak" (Bio-Security)**
- **Setting:** ISA outbreak spreading through region (Chilean crisis inspiration)
- **Goal:** Prevent infection, maintain 85% survival rate, don't lose license
- **Teaches:** Biosecurity, quarantine, vaccination strategy, early detection
- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Unlock:** Complete 2 previous scenarios

**5. "The Empire Builder" (Expansion)**
- **Setting:** Mid-size operation (3 licenses), goal: become regional leader
- **Goal:** Own 5 licenses, achieve 30% market share, vertical integration Tier 2
- **Teaches:** M&A strategy, competitor analysis, contract warfare, scaling operations
- **Difficulty:** Normal (but long, 5+ game years)
- **Unlock:** Complete 3 previous scenarios

**6. "Green Revolution" (Sustainability Challenge)**
- **Setting:** Low-sustainability farm (45 score), NGO pressure, media scrutiny
- **Goal:** Achieve 90+ sustainability score without bankruptcy
- **Teaches:** Sustainability investments, green tech, premium market access, reputation management
- **Difficulty:** Hard (high costs, tight margins)
- **Unlock:** Complete 4 previous scenarios

**7. "International Tycoon" (Global Expansion)**
- **Setting:** Domestic success, now expand to 4 international markets
- **Goal:** Export to Japan, USA, EU, China; earn 50M NOK annual revenue from exports
- **Teaches:** Export logistics, certifications, tariffs, international competition
- **Difficulty:** Expert
- **Unlock:** Complete 5 previous scenarios

**8. "The Perfect Season" (Nightmare Mode)**
- **Setting:** All systems unlocked, maximum difficulty, random disasters enabled
- **Goal:** Achieve 95+ sustainability, 100M NOK revenue, zero mortality, in ONE YEAR
- **Teaches:** Mastery of all systems, perfect execution, luck mitigation
- **Difficulty:** Nightmare
- **Unlock:** Complete all other scenarios

**SANDBOX MODE:**
- **Custom Start:** Choose starting capital (500K → 50M NOK), licenses (0-13 zones), technologies
- **Difficulty Sliders:** Disaster frequency, market volatility, AI aggression, regulatory strictness
- **Goal Setting:** Custom victory conditions (revenue, sustainability, market share, tech tree completion)
- **Random Seed:** Share seeds with community for identical challenge runs
- **Mod Support:** Load modded scenarios, custom maps, new mechanics

**Progression Unlocks:**
- **Technologies:** Unlock via scenario completion (RAS unlocks after "Green Revolution")
- **Regions:** New zones unlock after regional dominance scenarios
- **Difficulty Modes:** Nightmare mode unlocks after Expert scenario completion
- **Cosmetics:** Scenario-specific boat skins, building styles, logos

**Scenario Leaderboards:**
- **Speed:** Fastest completion time
- **Efficiency:** Highest profit/sustainability combo
- **Hardcore:** Fewest reloads, highest difficulty settings
- **Creative:** Best screenshot from scenario (community voted)

#### **Priority:** **P0** (Onboarding + retention critical)  
#### **Complexity:** **LARGE** (5-6 weeks, 8 scenarios + sandbox framework + balance + leaderboards)
