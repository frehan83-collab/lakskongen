## 2. About This Game (Long Description)

> Steam supports basic HTML: `<h2>`, `<br>`, `<b>`, `<i>`, `<ul>/<li>`, `<img>`. Below is written in Markdown for readability; convert to Steam HTML before pasting.

**Farming Simulator did it for tractors. Lakskongen does it for the ocean.**

Norway exports over $15 billion in salmon every year. The industry runs on cutting-edge science, brutal regulation, and razor-thin margins. Lakskongen puts you in charge of building your own salmon farming empire on the Norwegian coast — and it goes deeper than any farming sim you've ever played.

Start with a single pen in a sheltered fjord. Stock your first smolt. Learn to read water temperatures, manage feeding schedules, and keep sea lice below regulatory thresholds. Then grow. Expand across Norway's 13 real production zones, build processing plants, crack international markets, and outmaneuver AI competitors modeled on real industry giants. This is the full journey from struggling fish farmer to salmon king.

---

### The Core Loop

Every in-game week brings decisions that ripple through your entire operation:

**Stock → Grow → Harvest → Process → Sell → Reinvest**

Choose your smolt genetics. Optimize feeding for growth rate or feed conversion. Monitor disease pressure. Time your harvest for peak market prices. Process fish into up to 17 products — from whole gutted salmon to premium sashimi cuts to fish oil byproducts. Sell across 6 international market channels. Reinvest profits into better sites, technology, and expansion.

Every system connects to every other system. Genetics affect growth rates. Growth rates affect feed costs. Feed costs affect margins. Margins determine what you can invest in next. One bad decision can cascade. One great decision can compound.

---

### Key Features

🧬 **Real Salmon Biology**
Von Bertalanffy growth models, Mendelian genetics with 8+ heritable traits, feed conversion ratios that respond to temperature and stocking density. Your fish aren't numbers on a spreadsheet — they're biological systems that reward deep understanding.

🦠 **Complex Disease Management**
Sea lice develop treatment resistance. ISA and IPN spread through your sites via SIR epidemiological models. Algal blooms strike without warning. Choose between chemical treatments, cleaner fish, laser delousing, or preventive husbandry — each with real trade-offs.

🏭 **17-Product Value Chain**
Process whole fish into fillets, portions, smoked products, sashimi-grade cuts, and more. Extract value from byproducts: fish oil, fish meal, collagen, even fish skin leather. Every product has different margins, shelf life, and market demand.

📈 **6 Global Market Channels**
Trade on spot markets calibrated to real Fish Pool price data. Negotiate forward contracts with European retailers. Chase premium margins in Tokyo's sashimi market. Sell through 6 channels — from commodity spot to exclusive direct retail — each with unique risk/reward profiles.

⚖️ **Norwegian Regulatory Realism**
Navigate the traffic light system, maximum allowable biomass (MAB) limits, lice treatment thresholds, and environmental monitoring. Regulations aren't just background flavor — they're strategic constraints that shape every expansion decision.

🏢 **Empire Building in 5 Phases**
Progress from a single-site operator to an industry titan. Vertically integrate from hatchery to export. Take your company through IPO. Acquire competitors. Dominate the market across 5 distinct growth phases — each unlocking new mechanics and challenges.

🤖 **AI Competitors with Personality**
Compete against AI companies inspired by real industry players — Mowier, SalMax, Herøy Havbruk — each with distinct strategies, strengths, and market behavior. They'll undercut your prices, poach your sites, and lobby regulators. Outplay them all.

---

### Simulation Depth

Lakskongen is built on peer-reviewed aquaculture science and real industry data. This isn't a casual farming game with a fish skin — it's a serious simulation designed for players who want to understand how the world's most valuable seafood industry actually works.

- **Water chemistry** — Temperature, oxygen, salinity, and currents affect fish welfare and growth
- **Seasonal cycles** — Midnight sun summers and polar night winters change everything
- **Real pricing models** — Ornstein-Uhlenbeck mean-reverting price dynamics calibrated to historical salmon spot data
- **Technology progression** — Research your way from 1990s open net pens to cutting-edge semi-closed containment and offshore farms
- **100,000+ fish simulation** — Full biological modeling at scale, running smoothly at 60 FPS

### Multiplayer & Modding

Team up in **cooperative multiplayer** — share a company, split responsibilities, and build an empire together. One player manages sea operations while another runs the processing plant and market strategy.

**Full mod support** with planned Steam Workshop integration. Create and share custom scenarios, equipment, maps, and gameplay modifications. The community shapes the game's future.

## 4. Early Access Disclaimer

> Steam Early Access section — appears in the dedicated Early Access info box.

### Why Early Access?

"Lakskongen is the deepest aquaculture simulation ever attempted, and we want to build it with the community that will play it. Early Access lets us refine gameplay balance, simulation accuracy, and the user experience based on real player feedback — especially from players with industry knowledge who can help us get the details right."

### Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?

"We're targeting 6–12 months of Early Access before full release. The timeline depends on community feedback and the scope of features we add during this period."

### How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?

"The full release will add:
- Enhanced 3D fjord renderer with dynamic weather visuals
- Additional scenarios and challenge modes
- Steam Workshop integration for community mods
- Multiplayer polish and expanded co-op features
- Additional fish species and production technologies
- Balance refinements based on community feedback"

### What is the current state of the Early Access version?

"The core game loop is fully playable from egg to export. You can:
- Manage salmon through their complete lifecycle across Norway's production zones
- Process fish into 17 different products across the full value chain
- Trade on 6 international market channels with dynamic pricing
- Compete against AI companies with distinct strategies
- Navigate Norway's real regulatory framework
- Play in 6 fully localized languages (NO, EN, DE, FR, ES, JA)

The simulation systems, market mechanics, and business management features are complete and functional. We're now focused on visual polish, additional content, and refinements driven by player feedback."

### Will the price change after Early Access?

"We expect the price to increase at full release to reflect the additional content and polish. Early Access buyers will receive all updates at no additional cost."
