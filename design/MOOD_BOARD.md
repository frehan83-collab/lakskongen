# 🎨 Lakskongen Art Direction — Mood Board

## Overview
This mood board captures the essence of the "Nordic Illustrated" style for Lakskongen. It combines atmospheric elements, clean design principles, and a distinct Norwegian aesthetic to guide all visual development.

## Core Themes:
- **Atmospheric Fjords:** Deep, moody waters; majestic, snow-capped mountains; dramatic skies.
- **Clean Readability:** Uncluttered UI, clear information hierarchy, subtle visual cues.
- **Warm & Inviting:** Illustrated buildings with a sense of place; cozy, yet functional spaces.
- **Nature & Industry:** A blend of serene natural beauty with the functional elegance of aquaculture.
- **Understated Nordic Charm:** Drawing inspiration from Norwegian folk art and modern Scandinavian design.

## Visual Elements:

### 1. Color Palette Inspiration (Nordlys):
- **Deep Blues & Greens:** Reflecting the fjord's depth, the ocean, and northern skies.
- **Muted Earth Tones:** For mountains, land, and natural textures.
- **Warm Accents (Salmon Orange, Golden Yellow):** For highlights, alerts, and elements of salmon/sunlight.
- **Cool Whites & Greys:** For snow, ice, and clean UI backgrounds.

### 2. Lighting & Atmosphere:
- **Soft, Diffused Light:** Mimicking overcast Nordic skies or the gentle glow of the midnight sun.
- **Dynamic Day/Night Cycle:** Seamless transitions with realistic light shifts.
- **Aurora Borealis:** Magical light effects for northern regions at night.
- **Weather Effects:** Fog, rain, snow, and dramatic storm lighting.

### 3. Terrain & Environment:
- **Layered Parallax Mountains:** Creating depth in the background.
- **Illustrated Coastlines:** Stylized rocks, beaches, and vegetation unique to Norway.
- **Animated Water:** Subtle currents, gentle waves, and reflections.
- **Underwater View:** Cross-section showing depth, fish movement, and water clarity.

### 4. Buildings & Infrastructure (Illustrated Style):
- **Functional & Aesthetic:** Buildings that look like they belong in a Norwegian fjord setting.
- **Clean Lines with Soft Edges:** Avoiding harsh, blocky designs.
- **Subtle Details:** Indicating functionality without clutter.

### 5. UI Elements (Clean & Intuitive):
- **Frosted Glass/Translucent Panels:** Allowing the game world to show through subtly.
- **Illustrated Icons:** Clear, stylized icons for actions and information.
- **Elegant Typography:** Readable fonts with a Nordic touch.
- **Data Visualizations:** Charts and graphs integrated seamlessly, using the Nordlys palette.

## Inspirational References (Textual):

**Games:**
- **Dredge:** For its moody, atmospheric ocean and sense of place.
- **Mini Motorways / Islanders:** For clean, minimalist UI, soft gradients, and satisfying building aesthetics.
- **Anno 1800 / Frostpunk:** For dense, beautiful, and functional UI in management sims.
- **Terra Nil:** For environmental beauty and transformation.

**Photography & Art:**
- **Norwegian Fjord Photography:** Capturing the scale, light, and unique landscape.
- **Vintage Norwegian Travel Posters:** For stylized landscapes and color palettes.
- **Rosemaling / Traditional Norwegian Folk Art:** For subtle pattern and detail inspiration in UI or decorative elements.
- **Modern Scandinavian Design:** Emphasizing functionality, clean lines, and natural materials.
