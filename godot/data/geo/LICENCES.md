Not used: it is distributed only as 84 cells of 10 m GeoTIFF (many GB), and a photographic base reads as a GIS look against the Kyst & Stål art brief; the stylised base is built from N250 land cover and DTM 50 relief instead.
- **Geovekst 'Ortofoto Sentinel-2 Skyfri mosaikk'**: Norge digitalt-lisens (restricted to Norge digitalt parties): not usable in a commercial game.
- **Kartverket 'Høyde DTM Sømløs WCS'**: Norge digitalt-lisens: not usable; the open DTM 50 (CC BY 4.0) is used for relief instead.
- **Kartverket 'Sjøkart - Dybdedata' (FGDB)**: CC BY 4.0, but 2.6 GB; the same agency's depth grid is fetched from its WCS at 250 m instead.
- **Kystverket 'Hovedled og biled, arealavgrensning'**: NLOD 2.0, fetched and checked: 211 of 1050 active sea salmon sites lie inside these corridor polygons, so they are not exclusion zones; the buffered centre lines are used.
- **MET Norway NorKyst-800**: Not needed: the Copernicus Marine physics reanalysis (owner's login) gives the current layer; NorKyst would need large OPeNDAP pulls for a climatology.
- **BarentsWatch Fishhealth API**: Needs an API client registration (credentials): not used. The production-area boundaries come from Fiskeridirektoratet's open service instead.
