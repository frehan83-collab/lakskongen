-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AMBIENCE AND VERB SOUNDS (original work of the project)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Source: synthesised in the repository by tools/audio/gen_audio.py (roadmap
Au2-01) from seeded noise, sines and filters; encoded to Ogg Vorbis with
Blender's Audaspace. No third-party sample or recording is used, so there is
nothing to attribute. Every file's source, level and SHA-256 are in
godot/assets/audio/audio_manifest.json; what each one models is in
godot/assets/audio/AUDIO_SOURCES.md.

Ambience loops (60 s each): fjord_wind_light.ogg, fjord_wind_strong.ogg,
water_lap.ogg, gulls.ogg, feed_barge.ogg, boat_engine.ogg, rain_light.ogg,
rain_heavy.ogg, storm.ogg, harbor_ambience.ogg, underwater.ogg

Verb cues: verb_stock.wav, verb_feed_plan.wav, verb_delouse.wav,
verb_auction.wav, verb_storm.wav; and harvest_stinger.wav
(tools/gen_harvest_stinger.py, roadmap U2-05).

The Mixkit ambience files listed here before (ocean_waves, seagulls,
wind_light, wind_strong, rain_light, rain_heavy, storm, harbor_ambience,
underwater, ice_cracking) no longer ship: Au2-01 replaced every one.
