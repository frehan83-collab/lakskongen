## Tips for Great Screenshots

### Composition
- **Rule of thirds** - Place key elements on intersections
- **Leading lines** - Use UI elements or structures to guide eye
- **Depth** - Show foreground, midground, background
- **Framing** - Use natural frames (UI panels, structures)

### Lighting
- **Golden Hour filter** for warm, inviting shots
- **Storm Drama** for dramatic, moody atmosphere
- **Natural lighting** for realistic representation
- **Contrast** - Make sure important elements stand out

### Storytelling
- Each screenshot should answer: "What makes this game fun?"
- Show **progression** (tutorial → advanced features)
- Show **variety** (calm management → intense harvest)
- Show **polish** (beautiful UI, smooth gameplay)

### Technical
- **HUD visibility** - Toggle based on what you're showcasing
- **Filters** - Use sparingly, 2-3 max across all 10 shots
- **Motion** - Capture moment of action/activity when possible
- **Polish** - Wait for perfect frame (animations, timing)
