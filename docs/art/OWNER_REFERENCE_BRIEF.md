# Owner reference brief (2026-10-14)

The owner supplied five reference photos of Norwegian sea-pen farms, and later
land-based and offshore farms. They are third-party photos, kept privately by the
owner and never committed or shipped. This file describes them in words.

Owner direction, verbatim: "keep the graphics on a cool level with the user in
mind. best ever user and gaming experience based on research on other
simulation games that have success."

Read "cool" both ways: a cool Nordic colour temperature (blues, slate, snow,
granite) and a look that impresses. "With the user in mind" means readability
and comfort first: never sacrifice clarity, contrast or frame rate for effects.

## Rules for the 3D art

- **Palette:** cool water and land. Saturation is reserved for equipment and gameplay signals: buoy yellow #E8B92A, workboat red #C8102E, rope/net green #2E8B57, hose white #EDEFEF, HDPE black #1C1E20, pole blue #2F6DB5.
- **Water reads by mood:** sunny deep blue, calm-winter slate mirror, overcast grey. Calm water must reflect the sky and the mountains. Small chop shows sun glints. No glassy see-through water.
- **A site is not one ring.** A site is a grid of pens with a feed barge, white hoses to each pen, yellow mooring buoys, and optional bird nets on poles (central tent or rim poles), lice skirts and a centre feed spreader.
- **Vessels:** red/white workboats with cranes, a white-superstructure feed barge, wellboats.
- **Landscape:** steep granite walls with snow caps in winter; green/brown heath, birch and spruce on lower slopes in summer; a shoreline with a road and a small shore base near the farm.
- **Default camera:** ref 1's oblique aerial with the horizon in frame. Use ref 2's low oblique for the main menu and photo mode.

## Rules for the experience ("with the user in mind")

- Clarity first: every overlay is readable over every mood, and status is shown by colour plus shape.
- Calm by default: the cool palette plus restrained motion. Alarms are rare and meaningful.
- Learn from successful sims (Planet Zoo, Anno, Two Point, Frostpunk 2, Farming Simulator): the world is the home screen, verbs are one click away from the thing they act on, every action shows a preview and a payoff, and there is good pacing with clear goals.

## Second batch (2026-10-14): land-based and offshore farming

The owner sent two more sets titled "Land base farming" and "offshore farming": they want these as farm
types in the game, beside the open net-pen sites. All of them are described here in words (the photos are not in the repo).

| Ref | Scene | What to take |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Render, overcast: a land-based farm covering a granite peninsula | **~70 round tanks in two sizes** (large grow-out, smaller smolt/post-smolt), pale grey walls with **dark covered lids**, set in rows between **long, low white/grey service buildings** with roof vents. A **large dark-grey harvest/processing hall**. An **orange steel truss bridge on legs** carrying pipes/conveyor from a **concrete quay with a wellboat berth** to the tank rows. A **curved intake/outfall basin** at the shoreline. Parking, a road, a **small orange substation**. Rocky heath and cliffs, calm grey sea. |
| L2 | Photo at dusk (Indre Harøy-type hybrid flow-through farm) | ~12 big tanks with **banded walls** (grey over green/white) and flat white lids; an **orange truss conveyor bridge** into a tall grey building; a blasted granite pad; skerries; a pink-violet dusk sky over calm sea. | O1 | Diagram (Ocean Farm 1 concept) | **Safety-yellow, 8-sided semi-submersible steel frame** with a central control-room block. Systems to surface in UI: O2 and temperature sensors, subsea cameras, upward echo sounders, AIS, automation, motion reference, marine broadband to a **shore control centre**; DP service vessels, ROV, AUVs. |
| O2 | Render | The yellow octagonal ring: **columns and pontoons, diagonal bracing**, a walkway gantry across the middle, a white accommodation block, grey feed silos/towers, cranes, and the net cage hanging below the waterline. |

Art rules for these types:
- **Land sites** sit on blasted granite pads on the shore near the shore base: grey/white industrial volumes, dark lids, orange accents (pipe bridge, substation) as the only saturation. They read as a grid of circles from the default aerial camera, which echoes the pen grid on the water.
- **Offshore sites** sit in exposed water beyond the fjord mouth: one big safety-yellow structure, much larger than a pen (~110 m across), with a vessel alongside for scale. Swell and spray read stronger out there than in the fjord.
- Keep the same discipline: cool environment, saturated colour reserved for equipment and signals.
