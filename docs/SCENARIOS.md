Lakskongen — SCENARIOS & CAMPAIGNS

Overview

This document contains two main parts:
- Campaign Mode — a story-driven campaign in 5 chapters (Arven, Vekst, Industrialisering, Stormen, Fremtiden)
- Sandbox Scenarios — eight distinct starting scenarios with defined starting conditions, victory conditions, difficulty rating, key challenges, and estimated play time

Additionally, 10 Random Events are defined (with probability ranges and consequences) for in-game dynamism.

----------

CAMPAIGN MODE — Story-driven (5 chapters)

Chapter 1: Arven (The Inheritance) — 1985

Premise:
You inherit your grandfather's small salmon farm on a sheltered fjord in Trøndelag. Norwegian salmon farming is still young and experimental; there are few standards and most work is manual.

Starting state (scripted):
- Year: 1985
- Licenses: 1 (small coastal licence)
- Cash: 250,000 NOK
- Equipment: wooden pens (basic), manual feed sacks, small boat, no RAS/hatchery
- Staff: you (player) + 1 retired farmhand (NPC)

Core gameplay introduced:
- Manual feeding mechanics, simple growth cycles
- Basic water and mortality monitoring (visual checks, occasional netting)
- Local market sales mechanic (spot prices at local auction)
- Seasonal weather effects (winter storms reduce growth speed/mortality risk)

Objectives / End goal:
- Learn the core mechanics and reach the first profitable year.
- End condition for chapter: positive net income after the first production cycle and have at least 20% retained earnings.

Key lessons & story beats:
- Receive basic mentorship from NPC grandfather flashbacks; learn to balance feed costs vs. growth
- A small local inspection and permit renewal teach the player regulatory basics

Difficulty: Easy
Estimated play time: 1–2 hours

-----------------

Chapter 2: Vekst (Growth) — 1995

Premise:
Demand grows; you expand to multiple sites and face the first major disease crisis (IHN/ISA-like). Vaccination and better genetics start appearing.

Starting state (begin of chapter):
- Year: 1995
- Licenses: 2 (after buying one neighbouring small licence)
- Cash: 1,500,000 NOK
- Equipment: wooden + some steel pens, contract with a small feed supplier, logistics van
- Staff: 3 (you, 1 farm manager, 1 technician)

Core gameplay introduced:
- Multi-site management UI and scheduling
- Disease mechanics (infection probability, spread between pens/sites)
- Vaccination decisions (cost vs. efficacy)
- Export sales: contacts to EU buyers and price premiums for larger volumes

Major events & choices:
- An IHN outbreak hits a neighbouring farm; you must choose quarantine, cull, or treatment. Choices affect reputation and insurance eligibility.
- You can invest in selective breeding/genetic programs (long-term benefits)

Objectives / End goal:
- Reach 5 licenses and build or contract a small hatchery (either purchase or partner)
- Survive a full disease cycle and maintain positive cash flow

Difficulty: Medium
Estimated play time: 4–6 hours

-----------------

Chapter 3: Industrialisering (Industrialization) — 2005

Premise:
Scaling brings operational complexity. Sea lice become a major recurring problem and industrial solutions (baths, cleaner fish, mechanical delousing) appear. The player must choose technology investments and decide whether to forward-integrate into processing.

Starting state:
- Year: 2005
- Licenses: 5–8 (varies with player's previous choices)
- Cash: 15,000,000 NOK
- Equipment: larger pens, pumping systems, basic delousing (manual), contract with processor or option to build a processing plant
- Staff: 20+ (managers, technicians, onboard crews)

Core gameplay introduced:
- Supply chain & processing management (slaughter scheduling, cold chain)
- Sea lice simulation (seasonal peaks, treatment windows, resistance buildup)
- Capital investment decisions (buying a processing plant, investing in mechanization/automation)
- Market diversification: new buyers in USA and Japan with differing quality specs and transport costs

Major choices & trade-offs:
- Invest heavily in automation and R&D to reduce operational costs vs. slower expansion
- Build processing plant (higher upfront cost, better margins) vs. long-term reliance on processors

Objectives / End goal:
- Achieve >10 licenses and become vertically integrated (either own processing plant or long-term contracts guaranteeing capacity)
- Manage sea lice to keep mortality & downgrades within acceptable thresholds

Difficulty: Hard
Estimated play time: 8–12 hours

-----------------

Chapter 4: Stormen (The Storm) — 2015

Premise:
Political pressure, environmental activism, and media scrutiny intensify. Regulatory, tax, and public-relations events shape the industry. Offshore farming and consolidation (M&A) present new strategic directions.

Starting state:
- Year: 2015
- Licenses: 10+ (player has become major regional operator)
- Cash: 120,000,000 NOK
- Equipment: modern pens, partial automation, existing processing plant
- Staff: corporate structure with HR, PR, legal

Core gameplay introduced:
- Political & reputation mechanics (media stories, NGO campaigns)
- M&A mechanics (buying struggling competitors, managing integrations)
- Strategic choice: conventional coastal expansion vs. offshore pilot projects (higher cost, higher potential yield)
- Tax policy simulation (resource rent tax introduced; lobbying possible)

Major choices & events:
- Choose to spin up offshore pilot (requires significant CAPEX and new skillsets)
- Respond to an NGO exposé: transparency & remediation vs. denial/legal route
- Potential hostile takeover or opportunity to list company publicly

Objectives / End goal:
- Decide company future: go public (IPO) or consolidate as a private empire
- End-state metrics: market cap or net worth threshold, minimal regulatory compliance score, and reputation above critical threshold

Difficulty: Expert
Estimated play time: 10–15 hours

-----------------

Chapter 5: Fremtiden (The Future) — 2025+

Premise:
The industry faces climate change impacts, gene-editing debates, and a pivot to land-based RAS (recirculating aquaculture systems). The global market and ethical choices define the path to becoming "The Salmon King." 

Starting state:
- Year: 2025+
- Licenses: Player must choose where to allocate capital: maintain sea licences, invest heavily in land-based RAS, or expand internationally
- Cash: 250,000,000 NOK (depends on chapter progression)
- Equipment: mature processing, optional RAS prototypes, global distribution partnerships
- Staff: corporate + R&D + legal/ethics team

Core gameplay introduced:
- Land-based RAS simulation (energy cost, biosecurity, footprint)
- Climate-driven zone shifts and migration of best-growing waters
- Governance & ethics mechanics (gene editing/regulatory approval, public opinion, premium markets)
- Global expansion mechanics (foreign licences, trade barriers)

Major choices & endings:
- Adopt RAS (high CAPEX, long-term sustainability/brand premium) vs. continue optimizing sea-based operations
- Embrace gene-editing for growth/disease resistance (risk: public backlash, regulatory hurdles)
- Achieve status of "Lakskongen" by either market dominance, sustainable leader with net-zero footprint, or technological pioneer (multiple endings)

Objectives / End goal:
- Multiple possible endings: "Listed Salmon King" (IPO & market dominance), "Sustainable Monarch" (land-based + low-impact certification), "Innovation Titan" (major global IP & technology exports)

Difficulty: Legendary
Estimated play time: 12–20 hours (full campaign)

SANDBOX SCENARIOS (8 starting scenarios)

1) The Dreamer (Easy)
- Year: 2026
- Starting money: 50,000,000 NOK
- Licenses: 1 small coastal licence in a green (low-risk) zone
- Equipment: modern pens, contract hatchery
- Staff: 4
- Victory condition: Reach 100% growth efficiency (target kg per feed unit) and 2nd site within 2 years
- Difficulty: Easy
- Key challenges: ramp up production efficiently, hire correct crew
- Estimated play time: 2–6 hours

2) The Inheritor (Medium)
- Year: 1998
- Starting money: 500,000 NOK
- Licenses: 1 (older licence with backlog of maintenance)
- Equipment: aging wooden pens, high maintenance debt
- Staff: 2 (family-run)
- Victory condition: Become profitable and refinance debt within 18 months
- Difficulty: Medium
- Key challenges: manage cashflow, equipment failures, local politics
- Estimated play time: 4–8 hours

3) The Investor (Hard)
- Year: 2026
- Starting money: 300,000,000 NOK (capital only)
- Licenses: 0 (must acquire licences through purchase, negotiation, or joint ventures)
- Equipment: none
- Staff: none
- Victory condition: Secure at least 5 licences and operationalize three sites within 5 years
- Difficulty: Hard
- Key challenges: licence scarcity, regulatory approvals, time-to-market
- Estimated play time: 8–14 hours

4) The Pioneer (Expert)
- Year: 2022
- Starting money: 180,000,000 NOK
- Licenses: 0 sea licences; 1 experimental offshore/land-based development permit
- Equipment: prototype offshore cages or land-based RAS design (pilot scale)
- Staff: R&D-heavy team
- Victory condition: Demonstrate commercially viable offshore or land-based production and obtain full commercial licences
- Difficulty: Expert
- Key challenges: technical failures, severe CAPEX, regulatory hurdles, public scrutiny
- Estimated play time: 10–20 hours

5) The Turnaround (Hard/Time-pressure)
- Year: 2012
- Starting money: -50,000,000 NOK (company is effectively bankrupt)
- Licenses: 3 (at risk due to debt)
- Equipment: mixed, some assets under liens
- Staff: skeleton crew
- Victory condition: Survive 3 months (avoid licence revocation and reorganize) and secure refinancing or buyer
- Difficulty: Hard (time-critical)
- Key challenges: cashflow triage, emergency sales, creditor negotiations
- Estimated play time: 3–8 hours (intense)

6) The Organic (Medium)
- Year: 2026
- Starting money: 20,000,000 NOK
- Licenses: 2 (in zones allowing organic certification)
- Equipment: compliant feed/sourcing, stricter biosecurity
- Staff: trained in organic standards
- Victory condition: Achieve and maintain organic certification and reach premium market share within 3 years
- Difficulty: Medium
- Key challenges: lower stocking densities, premium brand building, certification compliance inspections
- Estimated play time: 6–12 hours

7) The Researcher (Specialist/Medium-Hard)
- Year: 2018
- Starting money: 30,000,000 NOK (grant funding)
- Licenses: Development/experimental licences only (no full commercial harvest)
- Equipment: R&D: small RAS facility, genetics lab access
- Staff: scientists and technical R&D staff
- Victory condition: Produce a validated innovation (vaccine, breeding strain, novel delousing tech) and secure commercial partner within 5 years
- Difficulty: Medium-Hard
- Key challenges: research failure risk, grant renewals, IP management
- Estimated play time: 8–16 hours

8) The Mogul (Very Hard)
- Year: 2015
- Starting money: 2,000,000,000 NOK
- Licenses: 20+ (large start)
- Equipment: state-of-the-art pens, processing, logistics
- Staff: corporate structure, legal, PR
- Victory condition: Maintain growth while surviving an industry-wide regulatory crackdown and retain at least 60% market share
- Difficulty: Very Hard (political + operations)
- Key challenges: regulatory exposure, activist campaigns, complex supply chains
- Estimated play time: 12–30 hours

RANDOM EVENTS (10)

Notes: Probabilities are per-site, per-year baseline unless otherwise noted. Effects may scale with farm size. Each event may have player choices that mitigate or amplify consequences.

1) Weather Storm (High probability in winter in exposed zones)
- Probability: 30% per-year (higher in exposed zones)
- Consequences: Pen damage (repair cost 0.5%–3% of asset value), temporary stock loss (0.5%–5% mortality), delayed harvest windows
- Player actions: invest in stronger pens (reduces probability/severity), emergency repairs, insurance claim

2) Disease Outbreak (Medium)
- Probability: 12% per-year (depends on biosecurity and region)
- Consequences: Elevated mortality (5%–40% depending on disease and response), market downgrades, mandatory culling/quarantine costs
- Player actions: vaccinate proactively (reduces chance/severity), cull, apply treatments (costly), request emergency funding

3) Sea Lice Epidemic (Medium-High)
- Probability: 18% per-year (seasonal spikes)
- Consequences: Increased mortality and downgrades, treatment costs (5%–20% of annual operating costs), growing resistance if same treatment used repeatedly
- Player actions: rotate treatments, use cleaner fish (initial cost & biological risks), invest in physical barriers

4) Market Shock — Price Crash (Occasional)
- Probability: 8% per-year global market shock; local shocks vary
- Consequences: Spot price drop (10%–50%) for a season; cashflow strain
- Player actions: hedge via forward contracts, diversify markets, reduce harvest volumes to wait for recovery

5) Export Opportunity (Low probability, high value)
- Probability: 6% per-year
- Consequences: Win a large export contract (guaranteed multi-year premium price). Requires quality standards and volume commitments.
- Player actions: invest to meet specs, expand production, choose contract term length (short/long)

6) Regulatory Change / Tax (Medium)
- Probability: 15% per-year chance of a regulatory change in regions with active politics
- Consequences: New compliance costs (0.5%–3% of revenue), potential licence reallocation or temporary moratoria on new licences
- Player actions: lobby, comply and adapt, pivot to other zones or products

7) Activist Exposé (Low-Medium)
- Probability: 7% per-year for large players / visible companies
- Consequences: Reputation hit (reduces premium on products, increases inspection frequency), potential fines, local protests that delay operations
- Player actions: PR campaign, transparency program (costs but recovers reputation), settle fines, improve operations

8) Tech Breakthrough (Low)
- Probability: 5% per-year (higher for R&D-focused scenarios)
- Consequences: New technology becomes available (e.g., low-cost RAS or novel delousing). Opportunity to license or adopt at cost. Early adoption gives competitive advantage.
- Player actions: invest in adoption, partner with researchers, pivot business model

9) Labor Strike / Crew Shortage (Medium-Low)
- Probability: 10% per-year in high-demand years
- Consequences: Reduced harvest efficiency (10%–40% slowdown), overtime costs, temporary inability to stock or harvest
- Player actions: hire contractors, improve wages/conditions, automate tasks

10) Biosecurity Leak (Rare but severe)
- Probability: 3% per-year (depends on biosecurity score)
- Consequences: Cross-site contamination, massive mortality at affected sites (20%–70%), reputational and regulatory scrutiny, large cleanup costs
- Player actions: full cull, emergency biosecurity measures, government assistance requests, long-term investments in biosecurity

DEPTH-SYSTEM CAMPAIGN SCENARIOS (2024–26 policy & events)

These four campaigns are registered in `src/LakskongenSim/Core/ScenarioSystem.cs`
and mirrored in `godot/data/scenarios.json`. Each one seeds a distinct
depth-system starting state via `ScenarioDefinition.ApplyStartingState` so the
disease, insurance, mitigation, containment/Smoltscreen and resource-rent
systems are exercised from day 0. Fields are additive: `objective`,
`starting_insurance`, `starting_mitigations`, `starting_blooms`,
`starting_disease`, `enable_resource_rent_tax`, and per-site `containment` /
`barrier`.

Every scenario also has a bankruptcy floor (S1-03, owner decision D16): the
default is -1 MNOK cash, and scenarios that start with a large stock carry a
working-capital facility in `godot/data/calibration.json`
(`finance.bankruptcyFloorByScenarioNOK`: po3_red_sone -15M, isa_utbrudd -24M,
algekatastrofe -26.5M, krise_manager -4.5M, lofoten_eventyr -2M). A three-rung
warning ladder (overdraft, bank warning at half the floor, final warning at
80%) comes first; measurements in docs/BALANCE.md §1e.

1) PO3 Rød Sone (`po3_red_sone`) — Difficulty 4, 3 years (1095 days)
- Hook: Production area PO3 flips to a red traffic light and the −6 % MTB cut
  bites; the only route to "miljøfleksibilitet" is closed-containment biomass
  credit.
- Objective: Recover MAB by operating at least one site at semi-closed
  containment and survive 1095 days in the red zone.
- Depth system: containment / environmental flexibility. `PO3 Nord` starts at
  `SemiClosed`; `special_rules` carry `containment_target = semi_closed` and
  `min_contained_sites = 1`.
- Restrictions: −6 % MTB on open sites; only semi-closed or higher qualifies;
  at least one site must stay contained.

2) ISA-utbruddet (`isa_utbrudd`) — Difficulty 5, 18 months (540 days)
- Hook: A confirmed ISA outbreak triggers a compulsory cull zone; biosecurity
  firewalls, insurance recovery and containment decide whether the neighbouring
  sites survive.
- Objective: Keep the ISA outbreak isolated to the core site, build Smoltscreen
  biosecurity between zones, and survive 540 days with catastrophe cover.
- Depth system: disease + insurance + Smoltscreen barrier. Seeds
  `starting_disease` (ISA) on `isa_kjerne`, pre-subscribes ISA and Mortality
  policies, and installs `Smoltscreen` barriers on both neighbouring sites.
- Restrictions: compulsory cull; firewalls required; outbreak-cover insurance
  required; outbreak must not spread to neighbours.

3) Algekatastrofen (`algekatastrofe`) — Difficulty 5, 1 season (365 days)
- Hook: A harmful algal bloom wipes out ~1,000 tonnes in a single event;
  early warning, mitigation (bubble curtain / O2 diffuser) and catastrophe
  insurance are the difference between survival and total loss.
- Objective: Install bloom mitigation on the exposed sites and survive the
  algal window (day 200–300) with catastrophe insurance.
- Depth system: bloom mitigation + insurance. Seeds a scripted `algae` bloom on
  `alge_sor`, pre-installs an O2 diffuser on `alge_indre` (pumping mirrored for
  `alge_midt`), and pre-subscribes a Bloom policy.
- Restrictions: early-warning system required; mitigation must be operational
  before the window; catastrophe insurance required.

4) Ressursrente-overgangen (`ressursrente`) — Difficulty 3, open-ended
- Hook: The 25 % resource-rent cash-flow tax with a 70 MNOK standard deduction
  reshapes profitability; scaling past the deduction makes every extra krone
  worth less.
- Objective: Stay profitable under the 25 % tax and reach 25 MNOK profit via
  value-chain investment.
- Depth system: resource-rent tax. `enable_resource_rent_tax = true` and a
  starting Mortality policy. Since S1-03 (owner decision D16) the tax is on in
  every scenario unless one sets `enable_resource_rent_tax = false`; the 70 MNOK
  deduction keeps small farms out of it. Measured: this scenario's starting farm
  (3 sites, 22 pens) never clears the deduction (rent base -25 to +27 MNOK a
  year), so the tax only bites once the player expands (docs/BALANCE.md §1e).
- Restrictions: 25 % cash-flow tax; the 70 MNOK standard deduction is shared
  across the business; profit above the deduction is taxed quarterly.

Gameplay tuning notes (design guidance for devs):
- Random event probabilities should scale with zone risk, farm size, and investments in mitigation. For example, better biosecurity reduces disease and leak probabilities; protected zones reduce storm damage.
- Difficulty scaling: larger starting capital increases exposure to political/regulatory events; smaller operators feel operational pressures and cashflow fragility.
- Victory conditions should be flexible: sandbox scenarios can have soft (qualitative) victories and hard (quantitative) achievements for leaderboards.
